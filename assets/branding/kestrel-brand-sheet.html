The idea

A falcon that holds still to watch

The kestrel is the one bird of prey that hangs motionless in the air — wings working, body locked to a point, eyes fixed on the ground below. It stays put and scans. That is the device: it sits in one place and reads the band around it, patient and exact. The mark is that posture seen head-on, wings spread, tail fanned for the hold.

Drawn as a single solid silhouette so it survives everywhere it has to live — a browser tab, a boot splash, and the 1-bit e-ink screen, where it reads cleanly in plain black or reversed white.
